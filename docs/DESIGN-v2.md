Adapted from the attached Brex style reference. **Colors stay E DIAS**; layout, density, radius, and single-accent rules follow the DESIGN.md system.

## Brand accents (unchanged)

| Role | Value | Maps from Brex |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Primary / ink / abyss | `#052e44` | Ink + Abyss + Carbon family |
| CTA ember | `#b6a571` | Ember `#ff5900` |

## Structure kept from DESIGN.md

- White concrete canvas, Fog banded sections
- 12px radius on cards, inputs, buttons (6px tags)
- No card drop shadows — Paper/Fog + 1px Mist borders
- Section gaps 48–80px; card padding 24–32px; max-width 1200px
- Single accent for primary CTAs only (gold)
- Compressed sans tracking; Cinzel only for display/hero personality
- Dark navy bookend footer
